Belle: original character by Ritsuko, drawn as an SVG rig (inline in index.html).
assets/js/belle.js animates the rig; the drawing itself is never altered.

Where she appears (homepage only)
1. Hero, beside the portrait (class belle-idle belle-portrait). Breathes, blinks, looks toward
   the pointer, occasionally waves, sniffs, wiggles or naps. Waves when hovered (hover devices only).
   Click, tap, Enter or Space: heart pops up and a short speech bubble appears.
   Greets once per browser session with "Hi! I'm Belle." (motion.js).
2. Analyst notebook (belle-working). Types, waves or sniffs when a notebook tab is chosen.
3. Walk strip above the contact band (belle-walking inside .belle-walk data-walk="once").
   When the strip scrolls into view she walks in, stops at data-stop (0.8 = 80% of the content
   width, above "Get in touch"), then waves and stays idle. Decorative and out of the tab order.

Motion rules
- Everything stops when the visitor presses Pause motion (header, every page; remembered across pages)
  or when the device has reduced motion turned on. The walk strip is hidden with reduced motion.
- Animation only runs while she is on screen and the tab is visible.

Unused assets kept in the repo (not loaded by any page): assets/images/belle-pose-1.png,
belle-pose-2.png (earlier hand-drawn poses), baby-panda/baby-seal/little-panda/little-seal SVGs.

New artwork that would improve her (please draw rather than generate):
- A side-view walk cycle (4 to 6 frames) and a sitting pose in the same line weight and palette,
  if you want a truer side walk than the current front-facing rig.
