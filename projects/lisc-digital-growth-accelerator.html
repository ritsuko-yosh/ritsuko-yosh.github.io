The business problem

Design a membership program that would help a Boston based client drive sales by bringing customers back more often.

Why it mattered

For a small business, keeping an existing customer is usually cheaper than winning a new one. A membership program is one of the few growth levers it can launch without a big budget, but only if it fits how the business already runs.

My role

I contributed to customer behavior analysis and the development of a tiered membership strategy as part of the consulting team.

I presented to the client and other teams on Zoom every Friday for seven weeks.

Data and tools

  • Customer visit information and operational context discussed during the client engagement; underlying records are not publicly shared
  • Run through the IXL Business Consulting Program as part of the LISC Digital Growth Accelerator

How I approached it

  • Scoped the goal with the client: more sales from existing customers.
  • Analyzed customer behavior to see who came back, who did not, and why.
  • Designed membership tiers that matched the client's operations, so the plan could actually be run.
  • Refined weekly using client feedback from the Friday presentations.

Findings

The analysis highlighted opportunities to encourage repeat visits through a membership offering designed around the salon’s operating constraints.

The team estimated the strategy could raise client retention by 15 to 20%. This is a projection made during the engagement, not a result measured after launch.

Business implications

The recommendation was a tiered membership program aligned with the client's operations. It placed 3rd of 36 teams for Best Action Plan to Drive Sales in August 2025.

Limitations

  • The 15 to 20% figure is an estimate. The engagement ended before any results could be measured.
  • Seven weeks allows a focused plan, not a tested one. A pilot would be the next step.

What this shows

Working with a real client, turning customer analysis into a program the business can run, and presenting recommendations every week to a non technical audience.
