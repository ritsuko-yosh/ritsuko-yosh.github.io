The business problem

Project PayPal's financial performance 12 quarters ahead, and test how much the forecast moves when key assumptions change.

Why it mattered

Forecasts feed budgets, hiring plans, and valuation. A single number with no sense of its range can push a whole plan off course.

My role

I analyzed historical financial information, explored seasonality using time-series regression, and worked with Holt-Winters forecasting and sensitivity analysis. The precise division of team responsibilities should be verified against the original report.

Data and tools

  • PayPal financial disclosures from SEC filings
  • Financial data obtained from SEC filings and PitchBook
  • Forecasting, regression, and financial statement analysis; software details to be confirmed from project files

How I approached it

  • Read the filings first to understand what drives PayPal's results.
  • Built a Holt-Winters model, which tracks level, trend, and seasonality.
  • Measured error with mean absolute percentage error (MAPE).
  • Ran sensitivity analysis to see how the forecast behaves as assumptions change.

Findings

The project reported a MAPE of 2.89%. The testing period and whether this was in-sample or out-of-sample have not been independently verified.

Sensitivity analysis explored how changes in financial assumptions could affect forecast outcomes; numerical scenarios are not reproduced without the original model.

Business implications

A tested forecast with a sensitivity range gives planners a base case plus a sense of how wrong it could be.

Limitations

  • Holt-Winters projects past patterns forward and cannot anticipate structural changes such as acquisitions, pricing shifts, or a downturn.
  • Uncertainty grows with the horizon. Quarter 12 is far less reliable than quarter 1.
  • If the 2.89% MAPE was measured on the same data used to fit the model, true out of sample error is likely higher.

What this shows

Time series forecasting grounded in financial filings, judging a model on measured error, and testing it under changing assumptions.
