The project

Build a 4 quarter financial statement forecast for PayPal covering Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, using PayPal SEC filings and PitchBook. The project included a full income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow forecast, plus ratio and DuPont analysis.

Why it mattered

A revenue forecast feeds the rest of a financial plan. Comparing methods and checking the forecast against reported results helps evaluate how well the model supports financial statement analysis.

My role

This was an individual Managerial Accounting course project completed from Sep 2025 to Dec 2025. I built the financial statement forecast, compared time series methods, and carried out ratio and DuPont analysis in Excel.

Data and tools

  • Data sources: PayPal SEC filings and PitchBook. The forecast used data through Q3 2025 only.
  • Tool: Excel.
  • Actual results for comparison: PayPal quarterly earnings releases.

How I approached it

  1. Compared 6 time series methods in Excel. Methods included Holt-Winters, ETS, exponential smoothing, Holt’s linear trend, and time series regression.
  2. Selected Holt-Winters multiplicative. I conservatively lowered its output by its 2.89% historical error.
  3. Built the financial statements. The project forecast the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow, and included ratio and DuPont analysis.
  4. Compared revenue forecasts with reported results. I evaluated each quarter’s error and the forecast MAPE.

Results

The revenue forecast came within 2% of PayPal's reported results in every quarter (0.9% MAPE), after I conservatively lowered the Holt-Winters output by its 2.89% historical error.

What the results mean

The adjusted revenue forecast came within 2% of reported revenue in each of the 4 quarters. The complete financial statement forecast and ratio and DuPont analysis placed those revenue estimates in a wider view of PayPal’s financial performance.

Limitations

The 0.9% MAPE describes the adjusted revenue forecast for Q4 2025 to Q3 2026. The 2.89% figure is the historical error used for the conservative adjustment, not the final forecast MAPE. The comparison does not establish accuracy for other periods or every forecast line item.
