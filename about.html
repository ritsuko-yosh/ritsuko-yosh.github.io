About

Analysis with a finance lens

I am an early career analyst with a business analytics degree and a finance degree in progress. What I enjoy most is the step between the numbers and the decision: working out what a result actually means for a business and saying it clearly to the person who has to act on it.

I grew up between Japan, Malaysia, and the United States, and I have lived in Boston since 2023. Moving between countries taught me to check my assumptions early, which turns out to be a useful habit in analysis too.

Approach

How I work on a problem

  1. 01

    Start with the decision

    Before touching data I write down who will use the answer and what they will do differently.

  2. 02

    Use the simplest method that works, then test it

    I compare options and report the error. If a simple model is nearly as good, I say so.

  3. 03

    Separate results from projections

    On this site I label what was measured, what was modeled, and what was projected.

  4. 04

    State the limits

    Every case study ends with what the analysis cannot tell you.
