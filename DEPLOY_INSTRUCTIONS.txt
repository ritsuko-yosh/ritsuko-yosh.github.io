ANIMATED PASTEL PORTFOLIO — Ritsuko Yoshida

1. Unzip the package.
2. Open the site folder.
3. Upload the CONTENTS of site (not the site folder itself) to the root of your ritsuko-yosh.github.io GitHub repository, replacing matching files.
4. Keep assets/images/portrait.jpg — it is your existing portrait.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to deploy, then hard-refresh the website.

Animations are CSS-based (peeking seal, gently wiggling panda, breathing seal, twinkling flowers, and entrance reveals). Reduced-motion accessibility is supported. All other existing pages and case studies are preserved.
