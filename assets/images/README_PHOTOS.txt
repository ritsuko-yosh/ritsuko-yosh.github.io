ADDING YOUR OWN PHOTOS

1. Portrait: name your professional photo portrait.jpg and place it in this folder (assets/images/). It will automatically appear on the homepage. Until then, the site shows an RY monogram rather than a fake headshot.
2. Project visuals: the four SVG files are abstract decorative artwork, NOT real charts or project results. Replace an SVG with your own exported image and update the matching HTML image filename if needed.
3. Use web-optimized JPG/WebP (ideally under 500 KB each) and do not include confidential client data or personal information.
4. Upload your changed files to the same paths in GitHub and commit.
