Case study in motion

Did the PayPal forecast hold up?

Built in fall 2025 on data through Q3 2025, then checked against what PayPal actually reported.

  1. 01 · Question

    Could a revenue forecast survive contact with real results?

    An individual Managerial Accounting project: forecast PayPal's statements for Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 using SEC filings and PitchBook.

  2. 02 · Method

    Six methods compared, one kept

    I tested 6 time series methods in Excel, selected Holt-Winters multiplicative, and conservatively lowered its output by its 2.89% historical error.

  3. 03 · Evidence

    Then the actuals came in

    Reported revenue from PayPal's quarterly earnings releases, placed against each forecast quarter.

  4. 04 · Finding

    Within 2% every quarter

    Errors of 1.0%, 0.3%, 0.5% and 1.8%, for a 0.9% MAPE across the four quarters.

  5. 05 · Limits

    What it does not prove

    The 0.9% applies to the adjusted revenue forecast for these four quarters only, not other periods or every line item.
